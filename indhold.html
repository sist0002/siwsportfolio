//business site - gabriella kauffmann

This was my first group project. We worked with a real business and made a re-design of their existing website.

We chose to colaborate with the artist Gabriella Kauffmann and our website therefore ended up being a digital art portfolio.

The website is developed in collaboration with Mie Lundberg, Zena Ibrahimi, Silja Bjørkvin and Silke Schultz.

click here to see the site!

PROCESS

//choosing a style

We worked a lot with our design concept and that the website should reflect the style of Gabriella's art.

Already early in the process, we had contacted Gabriella, who had been clear that it should be "an aesthetic that supports the artworks without drowning them out".

To give a few different takes on how the website could look, we therefore chose to each come up with a suggestion for a style tile. Afterwards, we spoke with Gabriella, who chose the last of the four.

//shared wireframes

We chose to spend a good part of our time on collaborating on making wireframes in Figma

In that way, everyone was on the same page with how the website should be structured and designed - and it secured that we would avoid misunderstandings later on in the process.

NEW KNOWLEDGE

//lottie

Lottie-files is the preferred file format for animations on websites.

While working on this project, I therefore learned how to animate in Adobe After Effects, as well as exporting to lottie and implementing it on your site.

//github

GitHub is a website, where you can share and collaborate on pieces of code.

We therefore used git, through GitHub, to be able to work on the site simultaneously. We each worked in our own branches and merged them into the base-branch at the end.
