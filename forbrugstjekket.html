//forbrugsrecap

As part of our solution we made an end of year campaign with cards. These cards includes user data imported from a database to personalise them.

To make the recap more engaging, we animated all the text and arranged the data in a graphic and easily comprehensible way.

At last, we made it possible to add these cards to your Instragram story, to encourage users to share them with friends and family and gain natural engagement with the campaign.

//green pass

Green Pass is essentially a point system, where the user gets rewarded for having sustainable consumption habits.

The more sustainable, the more points you get. These points can then be redeemed in shops to repair broken items like your bike or mobile phone.

As an extension for the exam project, I made the green pass concept into an app with a scannable QR-code.
