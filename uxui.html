//theme site - queer communities

With this project, I especially worked with prototyping, design and concept development.

My site focuses on queer communities in Denmark. It's purpose is to inform young queer people and their parents, while being a safe and including space. I chose this subject, because I am a volunteer at AURA at lgbt+ Denmark and I am therefore familiar with the target audience and how important spaces like these are.

Unfortunately, it is known that a large number of lgbt+ people in middle school have a poor well-being compared to their class mates, and as someone who was once in their shoes, it's a very important subject for me.

PROCESS

//developing ideas

I worked a lot with developing ideas, where i used the method "crazy 8's". I chose this method, to force myself to think fast and outside the box. By using this method, I actually came up with multiple ideas that ended up being central parts of my site!

I, for instance, got the idea of making a clickable map of Denmark. After using these methods, we would make heatmaps in the class I attended, where several people marked that specific feature of my site.

//from graphics to code

When I had finished my design in Figma, I had to implement my pretty organic design in coding format. I chose to split my backgrounds into 3 parts, one for header, main and footer.

In that way, I could transfer my design to the semanthics of coding in HTML and CSS.

NEW KNOWLEDGE

//prototyping in figma and thinking-out-loud testing

In Figma, I learned to make hi-fi wireframes of my site before starting to code.

Afterwards, I used them to make an interactive prototype, which I tested with thinking-out-loud testing. For this, I had several user journeys, that were relevant for my target audience, which the test subject had to follow.

For instance, they had to find a description of "transgender", which were in a subpage about labels. The test subject did not know what "labels" meant in a queer context, so afterwards I added a description, to make the site more accesible.

//lighthouse testing

I also learned how to make several tests of my site, including likert, 5 second and lighthouse.

Likert and 5 second are both user tests and their purpose is mainly to test the usability of the site.

Lighthouse, as shown above, indicates how fast and accesible the site is, as well as if your code is considered best practice. After testing my site for the first time, I optimized all my images and graphics to the webp format, to make my site more sustainable.
